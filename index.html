About us

Three crafts.
One team.

Estilo is a modelling, event entertainment and studio production agency. We supply professional models, create memorable live entertainment and produce fashion, brand and music content.

Estilo began with a simple observation: good talent often gets lost between the booking and the show. Models arrive without a clear brief, events have strong ideas but nobody steering them, and shoots lack a team that understands both. We built an agency that handles all of it properly.

Because the same people cast the models, book the artists and run the studio, nothing is lost in translation. You speak to us once, and we take care of the rest.

We would rather be remembered for how smooth it felt than how loud we were about it.
  • 01ReliableWe arrive early, prepared and ready to work.
  • 02ProfessionalEvery model and artist is briefed before the day.
  • 03ResponsiveOne point of contact with quick, clear replies.
  • 04Detail-mindedFrom styling to sound checks, we manage the small things.
